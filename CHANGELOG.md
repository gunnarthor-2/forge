# Changelog

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **1.3.1** | 13 Nov 2026 | DM's ruling: the characters are mostly strangers at the start; two friends joining together may build their characters with a shared past, kept the same on both sides. |
| **1.3** | 13 Nov 2026 | **The band** (`table/the-band.md`): the characters already built, with short stories and the **motifs already taken**, so each new character makes the party wider, not a twin. The Forge reads it before the opening (a pasted newer card wins), steers away from twin stories, and never shares another player's details. The hand-over now also includes the **player guide** and a **band update** draft. Seat 1, Ugo Fantozzi, added. |
| **1.2** | 13 Nov 2026 | From the first real build (seat 1): **when a player asks the Forge to choose**, it offers three options, the player picks, and the DM note records that the options were the Forge's. Also a tip for reading Archives of Nethys: list pages often fetch empty, so search for each entry by name. |
| **1.1.4** | 13 Nov 2026 | The Forge says plainly that it can't save anything to GitHub (the repository is public and holds no player's character). It hands everything over in the chat for the DM to pass to the campaign's scribe, and never claims to have saved. |
| **1.1.3** | 13 Nov 2026 | From a friend's test: every time the Forge offers options, it gives the two or three best fits **with the reason**, then always says there are more and offers the full list. |
| **1.1.2** | 13 Nov 2026 | DM's rule: **no flight at creation**. No flying ancestries, heritages or fly-Speed feats, and no flying companions or mounts. Ground mounts are allowed, with their size and action costs explained. Anything close to flight goes to the DM. Flying familiars are fine and get flagged. |
| **1.1.1** | 13 Nov 2026 | DM's request: the opening now tells the player plainly that this is a **long campaign, not a one-shot** (maybe a couple of years), so the character is one they may play for a long time, and that's why the build takes its time. Plus a "take your time" line under How you talk. |
| **1.1** | 13 Nov 2026 | DM's direction: the Forge's job is **hooks**, things the DM can write story from. **The eight become the ten**, in two groups. *The hooks* are what drives them, what's at stake (someone or something they still have and fear to lose; new), the open wound, items, and connections (named people with an ally, plus a strong feeling about a people, a god, a devil or a beast). *The person* is the scene, the count, the line, the crack and the open question. **What drives them must live in the person, not in one place.** **Seeds replaced by twelve hook seeds** (`campaign/hooks.md`): The Missing · Taken by a God · The Cure · The Devil's Due · The Crown · The Rival · The Beast · The Silent God · The Oath · The Life Debt · The Grudge · The Keeper. Each pushes for specifics: who, what, when, alive or not. **Go as far as the player can.** Stage 3 asks what's at stake. The DM note now has a hook list sorted into the five kinds. |
| **1.0.1** | 13 Nov 2026 | DM's rulings: starting wealth is the **item method** only (one 2nd-level and two 1st-level permanent items plus 25 gp); **marked Nephilim** allowed, meaning a character touched by a devil rather than born to a Tiefling, at their ancestry's points + 1. |
| **1.0** | 13 Nov 2026 | First version in its own repository, rebuilt after the DM's test of the previous Forge (v2). **Guide-first:** the Pathfinder build is the spine again (picture → class → ancestry → background → attributes → skills and feats → spells → gear → the heart → portrait), and each stage teaches the choice before the player makes it, with one or two story questions growing out of it. **Newcomer rule:** every question can be answered without knowing the game or the world, and comes with example answers. **The eight** (reason · named people · scene · mark · count · line · crack · open question) are collected along the way and checked at stage 9. **Seeds** are optional, offered only at stage 9, and rewritten as plain situations and questions with no symbols or groups; **sparks** likewise rewritten. The **heroes rule** stays (kind or neutral, never villains). **Rules split out** into `campaign/rules.md`, with level-3 wealth confirmed on Archives of Nethys (GM Core, Treasure for New Characters). New **plain-words guide** and **class chooser**. One **example portrait**. Party card and DM note as templates. The world digest is no longer part of the Forge: the Forge knows nothing of the campaign's world. |

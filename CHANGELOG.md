# Changelog

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| **1.0.1** | 13 Nov 2026 | DM's rulings: starting wealth is the **item method** only (one 2nd-level and two 1st-level permanent items plus 25 gp); **marked Nephilim** allowed, meaning a character touched by a devil rather than born to a Tiefling, at their ancestry's points + 1. |
| **1.0** | 13 Nov 2026 | First version in its own repository, rebuilt after the DM's test of the previous Forge (v2). **Guide-first:** the Pathfinder build is the spine again (picture → class → ancestry → background → attributes → skills and feats → spells → gear → the heart → portrait), and each stage teaches the choice before the player makes it, with one or two story questions growing out of it. **Newcomer rule:** every question can be answered without knowing the game or the world, and comes with example answers. **The eight** (reason · named people · scene · mark · count · line · crack · open question) are collected along the way and checked at stage 9. **Seeds** are optional, offered only at stage 9, and rewritten as plain situations and questions with no symbols or groups; **sparks** likewise rewritten. The **heroes rule** stays (kind or neutral, never villains). **Rules split out** into `campaign/rules.md`, with level-3 wealth confirmed on Archives of Nethys (GM Core, Treasure for New Characters). New **plain-words guide** and **class chooser**. One **example portrait**. Party card and DM note as templates. The world digest is no longer part of the Forge: the Forge knows nothing of the campaign's world. |

# Hook seeds

*For the Forge. Hooks are what the DM writes the story from. Every character leaves the Forge with **five kinds** (FORGE.md, "The ten"): what drives them, what they're afraid to lose, the wound that's still open, items, and connections. Most come out of the build questions. These seeds are a menu for **stage 9**: offer two or three that fit what the player has already said, in your own words, and let them take one, bend one, combine one with their story, or say no. Prefer seeds not yet taken at this table (the party card says which).*

*Whichever seed they take, **push for specifics**: who, what, when, and whether they're alive. "My sister went missing" is not finished yet. "Ilsa, twelve, gone from the mill road four winters ago; only her shoe was found" is. Never answer a seed's mystery yourself. That's the open question, and it belongs to the DM.*

*Every seed lives in the person, not in one place, so it goes wherever a sellsword goes.*

---

### 1 · The Missing
Someone they love vanished a few years ago: no body, no word, no one who will say what happened.
- *Make it specific:* Who? How old were they? Where were they last seen, and what was left behind?
- *It drives them to:* search every town, ask every stranger, and never stop.

### 2 · Taken by a God
A god, or that god's priests, took someone from them: a sacrifice, a calling, a miracle with a price.
- *Make it specific:* Which god, in their words? What happened that day? Do they hate the god, or want to understand?
- *It drives them to:* get them back, get an answer, or make the god pay.

### 3 · The Cure
Someone they love is cursed, turned to stone, sleeping and won't wake, or changed into something else. Somewhere there's a spell, or a person, who can undo it.
- *Make it specific:* Who, and what happened to them? Where are they now, and who watches over them?
- *It drives them to:* find the cure, whatever it costs.

### 4 · The Devil's Due
A bargain was made with a devil, by them or by a parent. Something was given and something is still owed, and the bill hasn't been paid.
- *Make it specific:* Who made the bargain, and for what? What's owed? Does the devil have a name they know?
- *It drives them to:* pay it, break it, or outrun it.

### 5 · The Crown
They believe a title, land or throne is rightfully theirs: stolen, lost, or never granted.
- *Make it specific:* What exactly, and who sits on it now? Who else believes them?
- *It drives them to:* win it back, or prove they deserve it.

### 6 · The Rival
Someone alive is better than them at the thing they care about most, and wants the same prize.
- *Make it specific:* Name, people, trade. What happened the last time they met? What do they both want?
- *It drives them to:* beat them, once and in front of everyone.

### 7 · The Beast
One creature: they hunt it, they love it, or they swore to find it.
- *Make it specific:* What kind, in their words? What did it do, or what did it mean to them? Have they seen it?
- *It drives them to:* follow every rumour of it.

### 8 · The Silent God
They pray every night and have never once been answered. Or they're angry at a god and say so out loud.
- *Make it specific:* Which god, in their words? What did they ask for? What would an answer look like?
- *It drives them to:* earn an answer, or demand one.

### 9 · The Oath
They made a promise to someone who was dying, and it isn't kept yet.
- *Make it specific:* Who, and what were the exact words? Why haven't they kept it?
- *It drives them to:* keep it, even when it costs too much.

### 10 · The Life Debt
Someone from another people saved their life: an Orc, an Elf, a Goblin, a stranger. Now they owe that whole people.
- *Make it specific:* Who saved them, from what? Did they ever get to say thank you?
- *It drives them to:* repay it, to anyone of that people who needs it.

### 11 · The Grudge
They distrust a whole people because of something that happened, and the story is going to test that.
- *Make it specific:* Which people, and what happened? Is there one of them they secretly don't hate?
- *It drives them to:* be right, or be proven wrong. *(Rule 6: this is a crack they can grow out of, never cruelty.)*

### 12 · The Keeper
They guard something for someone else: a key, a sealed letter, an heirloom that was never theirs. They don't know everything about it.
- *Make it specific:* What is it, and who gave it to them? What were they told to do with it, and when?
- *It drives them to:* keep it safe until the right day, or the right person.

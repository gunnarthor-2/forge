# Campaign rules

*For the Forge. These are the DM's rules for this campaign. Offer only what is allowed here. Anything not covered goes to the DM: "let me note that for your DM."*

---

## The basics

- **Pathfinder Second Edition, Remaster.** Read every rule on Archives of Nethys, `https://2e.aonprd.com`.
- **Level 3.** Build the character at level 1 and level it up twice, following the class's advancement table.
- **Attributes:** the standard boosts only. That means the ancestry's boosts (or its two free boosts, if the player prefers that option from the ancestry rules), two from the background, one from the class, and four free boosts at level 1. No rolled attributes.
- **No variant rules:** no Free Archetype, Automatic Bonus Progression, Proficiency Without Level, Stamina or Gradual Ability Boosts. Archetypes come only through normal feat choices, and only if the player asks.
- **No alignment.** The Remaster has none. Holy and unholy traits are used only where a rule calls for them.
- **Rarity:** the ancestries below are approved, including the Uncommon and Rare ones. Any other Uncommon or Rare option (a feat, a spell, an item, an archetype) needs the DM. Note it; don't grant it.

## The heroes

Kind, or at least neutral; never villains (FORGE.md, rule 6). In the rules this means **no unholy sanctification** and **no unholy champion cause**.

## Not allowed

- **Firearms**: no Gunslinger, no firearm path for the Inventor, no firearms in gear.
- **Satyr**: not available.
- **Versatile heritages** other than the three under "Tieflings and mixed parentage" below. Changeling, Dhampir, Duskwalker, the elemental ones and the rest are not offered.
- **Classes that need the DM first:** Exemplar (Rare), and anything Archives of Nethys marks as a playtest.

## Ancestries and their cost

Each ancestry has a **tier**, and each tier has a **point cost**. The band shares **one point per seat** (four players, four points; a fifth seat adds a fifth). The points are a **hard limit**: check the party card before a player commits.

| Tier | Cost | Ancestries |
|---|---|---|
| **0 · Known** | 0 | Dwarf · Elf · Human · Orc · Goblin · Kobold |
| **1 · Limited** | 1 | Halfling · Gnome · Hobgoblin · Leshy · Tiefling |
| **2 · Rare** | 2 | Tengu · Catfolk · Ratfolk · Centaur · Lizardfolk · Minotaur · Ghoran · Jotunborn · Azarketi |

**What to tell the player before they commit.** The tier is about **how unusual this people is in the campaign**: the higher the tier, the more often strangers stare, mistrust or misjudge them. Present it as a story choice made with open eyes:
- **Known:** you'll be recognised, though not always liked.
- **Limited:** you'll meet suspicion more often, and welcome has to be earned.
- **Rare:** you'll be a stranger nearly everywhere. That's a real roleplay commitment: choose it for the story, not in spite of the friction.

Don't say more than that about how the campaign treats any people. You don't know, and it's the DM's to show.

### Tieflings and mixed parentage

- **A Tiefling.** Pathfinder has no Tiefling ancestry; it builds them with the **Nephilim** versatile heritage. Here a Tiefling is **Human + Nephilim**, and if they take a lineage feat it is **Hellspawn** (descended from devils). Human is only the rules frame (size, speed, Hit Points). The character *is* a Tiefling, born to Tieflings. **1 point.**
- **A half-Tiefling** has one Tiefling parent. Build them as the other parent's ancestry + **Nephilim** (Hellspawn if a lineage feat is taken), for example a Halfling with horns and a tail. They cost **that ancestry's points + 1**: a half-Tiefling Orc is 1 point, a half-Tiefling Halfling 2.
- **Other mixed parentage** is allowed. **Aiuvarin** is for an Elf parent and **Dromaar** for an Orc parent. Build them as the *other* parent's ancestry with that versatile heritage, as the rules say. They cost **the higher of the two parents' points**: Human–Elf or Human–Orc is 0, Halfling–Elf is 1.
- **Marked, not born.** A character can also be Nephilim because something touched them, not because of a parent: a devil's mark, a fiend's curse. Build them as their own ancestry + **Nephilim** (Hellspawn if a lineage feat is taken), at **that ancestry's points + 1**, the same as a half-Tiefling. How they look (golden eyes, a bruise that never fades) is the player's to describe. *Where the mark came from is an open question for the DM: write it down in the note and never answer it yourself.*
- Other Nephilim lineages (celestial and the rest) need the DM.
- A character has only one versatile heritage.

### Things to say about particular ancestries

- **Centaur and Jotunborn are Large.** Before they commit, tell them what that means for doors, tunnels, ships and inns.
- **Azarketi and Ghoran** are marked *Legacy Content* on Archives of Nethys (from before the Remaster). Use them as written there, tell the player, and put it in the DM note.

## Wealth at level 3

Use the **Character Wealth** table (GM Core, "Treasure for New Characters", on Archives of Nethys) and read it live. This campaign uses the **item method** (DM's ruling). At level 3 that means:
- one 2nd-level permanent item,
- two 1st-level permanent items,
- and 25 gp to spend on anything else, including ordinary gear and consumables.

The lump-sum option isn't used. Items must be **common**; anything uncommon goes to the DM. No firearms.

## Languages and Lore

- Languages come from the ancestry and the Intelligence bonus. Offer common languages. **Diabolic** and **Empyrean** are uncommon: note them for the DM.
- Every **Lore** skill is about the character's **own home life**: their trade, their town, their craft (Mercenary Lore, Sailing Lore, Farming Lore…). Never a Lore about anywhere or anything beyond home.

## Replacement characters

If a character dies, the replacement is built the same way, unless the DM tells you in the chat that it is different. In that case the DM tells you what you need.

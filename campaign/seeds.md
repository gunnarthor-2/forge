# Seeds: optional story questions

*For the Forge. These are optional. The DM wrote them because the questions they ask suit this campaign. Offer them only at stage 9, once the character already has a story of their own. Pick two or three that **fit what the player has already told you**, preferring any not yet taken at this table (the party card says which), and tell each in your own words, fitted to the person. A seed adds to the player's story. It never replaces it. "No thanks" is a complete answer.*

*Each seed is a situation and a question. Use the player's own names, places and details. Don't add anything to a seed: no symbols, emblems, names of groups or signs that the seed doesn't give. Keep it as plain as it is written.*

---

### 1 · The Trusted Counsel
Someone came into your family's life as a friend (an advisor, a steward, a priest at your parent's elbow) and made themselves indispensable. Year by year the house stopped being yours: decisions made in rooms you weren't in, loyal servants sent away, your family smiling at the one who was emptying them out. You left to become strong enough to come back.
- *The question:* What would you do to the person who stole your family without ever drawing a blade?
- *Make it yours:* Who is still inside that house? What name does the counsellor go by?

### 2 · The Marker
You lost at a gaming house and signed for a debt you couldn't pay, and then you ran. The house has long arms and a long memory. Now and then someone in a tavern looks at you a moment too long.
- *The question:* How much is your freedom worth, and who would pay it for you?
- *Make it yours:* What did you bet, and why couldn't you stop? Who signed for you?

### 3 · The Outlaw Company
Between contracts you once rode with a company that did justice outside the law. In a town being strangled by a moneylender, they burned his ledgers, freed his debtors and were gone before the law arrived. You never joined them, and you still haven't decided whether they were heroes or arsonists.
- *The question:* Is it right to do a terrible thing to someone who holds far too much?
- *Make it yours:* What did you do that night: help, watch or stop them? Who in that company do you still think about?

### 4 · The Unfinished Lesson
Your teacher (in magic, in scholarship, in some craft only a few understood) went deeper than anyone was supposed to go. The letters grew strange, then stopped. People came asking where your teacher was, and they did not look like friends. You still have the last letter, and you have never been able to finish reading it.
- *The question:* Some knowledge is dangerous. Is it worth having anyway?
- *Make it yours:* What did your teacher study? What did the letter say, as far as you got?

### 5 · One Night of Fire
The street you grew up on is gone. One night, one fire, and in the morning there was nothing left. Nobody was ever made to answer for it. Some say it was an accident, and you've stopped believing in accidents.
- *The question:* What do you owe the people who didn't get out?
- *Make it yours:* Where were you that night, and why weren't you home? Who else survived?

### 6 · What the Forest Let Go
You were lost in an old forest once (hunting, fleeing, or just lost), and something in there decided to let you live. You don't talk about what you saw. You don't go into deep woods now without asking permission first, out loud, and you've never been able to explain why it works.
- *The question:* What do you owe to something that spared you?
- *Make it yours:* What did it look like, or did you never see it? What did it take from you in return?

### 7 · The March You Don't Speak Of
You marched once in a great army, young and proud, and on that march the army did something to people who had done nothing to you. You have never spoken of it. You left the colours afterwards, and you fight for coin now because coin doesn't lie about what it's for.
- *The question:* Can you make up for what you did when you were following orders?
- *Make it yours:* What did you do, and what did you refuse to do? Who from that army still looks for you?

### 8 · The Contract You Couldn't Read
You grew up working under a contract you couldn't read, in a mine, a mill or a dockyard owned by people who lived far away and never came. The terms got worse every year. Someone taught you to read at night, and the day you read your own contract you understood everything. You left. Others didn't.
- *The question:* Do you owe something to the ones still working under those terms?
- *Make it yours:* Who taught you to read? What did the contract say that made you go?

### 9 · Spared
You saw a killing in an alley once, quick and quiet. In the dark the killer turned and *looked* at you, and then let you go. You have never understood why, and that frightens you more than the killing.
- *The question:* What do you do when evil lets you go?
- *Make it yours:* Who died? Did you ever tell anyone what you saw?

### 10 · The Box That Won't Open
You inherited a small box, old and beautifully made, that is impossible to open. Locksmiths have tried, and magicians have frowned at it and handed it back. It's warm sometimes. Whoever made it is long dead, and you've been told the craft is lost. You keep it with you, and you've never asked yourself why you can't sell it.
- *The question:* What is worth protecting so well that no one can ever reach it?
- *Make it yours:* Who left it to you, and what did they say? What do you think is inside?

### 11 · The Challenge
Where you come from, a quarrel can be settled in the ring, in front of everyone, and the loser lives with it. You were challenged once. You won when you should have lost, or lost when you should have won, or walked away and were called a coward for it. That fight still follows you.
- *The question:* What is strength for?
- *Make it yours:* Who challenged you, and over what? What did everyone say afterwards?

### 12 · The Paid Miracle
You owe your life, or someone's, to a healer who charged for it: a temple, a famous hand, a house of healing that cured the rich and turned the rest away. You've seen what it did for you, and you've seen what it cost the people who couldn't pay.
- *The question:* Can something be good and bought at the same time?
- *Make it yours:* What did the healer do for you? What did you see that you can't forget?

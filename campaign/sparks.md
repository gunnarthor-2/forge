# Sparks — for a blank page

*For the Forge. Use these only when a player arrives with nothing ("I don't know — something cool?"). A spark is a working life to start from: what this sellsword did before the band, plus one odd detail worth asking about. It is not a story. Offer **three**, chosen for variety and with at least one light one, retold in your own words. When one catches, carry on with the build; the questions grow it from there. Every spark is set in the character's own home, like everything else. Not every sellsword is haunted, and some of these are meant to be fun.*

---

- **The Race Rider** *(light)*. You rode animals in races for gamblers at fairs (ponies, dogs, anything that would run) until you grew too big, too old or too honest. You know every way a race can be fixed.
  *Ask:* What did you ride? Which race did you throw, and which one wouldn't you throw?
- **The Ship's Bouncer** *(light)*. You kept order on passenger ships: drunks, card cheats, and one stowaway you never reported. The captain had rules and meant them, and you liked that.
  *Ask:* Why did you leave the sea? What happened to the stowaway?
- **The Carver's Mule**. You hauled stone, bone and once a little silver for an old carver of luck-charms, the kind people hang over doors and cradles. You learned the carver's sayings but never the craft.
  *Ask:* What did the carver say the charms were for? What did you carry that you weren't meant to look at?
- **The Wall Shift**. You stood militia on a town wall: your shift, your stretch of stone, your name scratched into it. You left over a quarrel about who the wall answers to.
  *Ask:* Who was right? Who's standing your shift now?
- **The Magician's Bodyguard** *(light)*. You guarded a travelling magician who had more enemies than spells and far more charm than sense. You were there for the day the charm didn't work.
  *Ask:* What happened that day? What was your employer really afraid of?
- **The Weapon You Can't Afford**. You carry a weapon far finer than anything a sellsword could buy. It was won, given or taken, and somebody wants it back.
  *Ask:* How did you get it? Who wants it, and what would they do to have it?
- **The Price on the Lead Wagon**. Your family hires its children out as guards, generation after generation, and is proud of it. You're tired of being a price.
  *Ask:* What does your family expect of you? What would you be if you could choose?
- **The Second Child**. As a child you were sent to be raised in another house, as a pledge of peace between two families. You came back a stranger to both.
  *Ask:* What did the other house teach you that your own never could? Who there still writes to you?
- **The Company Cook** *(light)*. You fed a mercenary company for years. The night the camp was raided, you were the one still standing over the pot with a cleaver.
  *Ask:* What's your best dish? Who in the company do you still cook for, in your head?
- **The Edge of the Map** *(light)*. You guarded surveyors and mapmakers on long trips to the edges of the known lands. Every map you carried ended in the same blank, and you've always wanted to see past the last line.
  *Ask:* What's the farthest you went? What did the old guides say was out there?
- **The Healer Without Leave**. Where you grew up, magic was against the law, and you healed anyway: at night, for neighbours, a little at a time. Someone talked.
  *Ask:* Who did you save? Who talked, and do you know why?
- **The Week We Held the Tavern** *(light)*. You and a few friends once held a tavern for a whole week against a landlord's bullies. It was the best week of your life.
  *Ask:* Who was with you? How did the week end?

# Class chooser

*For the Forge. Use it to turn what a player says they want into **two or three classes to look at**. Each line is a rough plain-words sketch to help you choose what to offer, nothing more. Before you describe a class to the player, read its page on Archives of Nethys and describe what the page actually says. A class not allowed in this campaign is marked; see `campaign/rules.md`.*

---

## Step 1 · Listen for the wish

| The player says something like… | Look first at |
|---|---|
| "hit things hard", "the big one at the front" | Fighter · Barbarian · Champion · Guardian |
| "protect my friends", "the wall" | Champion · Guardian · Fighter (with a shield) |
| "smart and dangerous", "always has a plan" | Investigator · Ranger · Commander · Thaumaturge · Inventor |
| "hunt monsters", "know their weak spot" | Ranger · Thaumaturge · Investigator |
| "sneaky", "quick", "knives in the dark" | Rogue · Swashbuckler · Ranger |
| "a bow", "shoot from far away" | Ranger · Fighter · Rogue |
| "charming", "talk my way out", "the face" | Bard · Swashbuckler · Rogue · Sorcerer |
| "lead the band", "tactics" | Commander · Bard · Fighter |
| "heal people", "keep everyone standing" | Cleric · Oracle · Druid · Bard · Witch · Animist |
| "real magic", "blow things up" | Wizard · Sorcerer · Witch · Kineticist · Psychic |
| "magic *and* a sword" | Magus · Champion · Warpriest Cleric · Thaumaturge |
| "nature", "animals", "the wild" | Druid · Ranger · Kineticist |
| "a companion" (animal, spirit, creature) | Druid · Ranger · Summoner · Champion · Witch (a familiar) |
| "fight with my fists" | Monk · Fighter |
| "potions, bombs, tinkering" | Alchemist · Inventor |
| "strange powers of the mind" | Psychic · Witch · Oracle |
| "spirits", "speak with the other side" | Animist · Oracle · Witch |
| "carve runes, write power into things" | Runesmith |
| "raise the dead to fight for me" | Necromancer *(see the note under Step 2)* |

## Step 2 · What each class is like to play (a quick sketch; check the page)

- **Alchemist**: makes bombs, potions and mixtures every day; a clever helper with a sling of explosives.
- **Animist**: a divine caster who works with spirits that share their power.
- **Barbarian**: fury in a fight; hits very hard and takes punishment, at the cost of some defence.
- **Bard**: magic through music, words and performance; helps the whole band, and a natural face.
- **Champion**: a holy warrior sworn to a cause; heavy armour, protects allies, punishes enemies. *(In this campaign, never an unholy cause.)*
- **Cleric**: a priest with divine magic; the strongest healer. Some are frontline warpriests.
- **Commander**: a battlefield tactician who gives orders that move and empower the whole band.
- **Druid**: primal magic; nature, animals, sometimes shapeshifting.
- **Fighter**: the best with weapons; simple to learn and hard to beat. A great first class.
- **Guardian**: an armoured defender who draws enemies' attacks and shields friends.
- **Inventor**: a tinkerer with one great invention (armour, a weapon or a construct). *(In this campaign, never a firearm.)*
- **Investigator**: a detective who studies a foe, then strikes exactly where it hurts.
- **Kineticist**: commands an element (fire, water, earth, air, wood or metal) as their own weapon.
- **Magus**: mixes spells and weapon blows in the same strike.
- **Monk**: unarmed fighting, speed and discipline.
- **Necromancer**: commands thralls of bone and death magic. *The heroes rule still holds: a decent person who uses a dark craft. If a player wants this, say plainly that you'll note it for the DM.*
- **Oracle**: divine magic from a mystery that costs them something; powerful and strange.
- **Psychic**: magic of pure thought; raw power from the mind.
- **Ranger**: a hunter and tracker who marks one prey and takes it apart; bows or blades; often a companion.
- **Rogue**: skills, sneak attacks and tricks; the most skilful class.
- **Runesmith**: traces runes on weapons, allies and enemies, then calls their power at the right moment.
- **Sorcerer**: magic in the blood; fewer spells, more power, and a bloodline that shapes them.
- **Summoner**: bonded to a powerful creature (an eidolon) that fights beside them.
- **Swashbuckler**: flair and style; daring moves build into a big finishing blow.
- **Thaumaturge**: uses charms, relics and folk knowledge to find and hit a creature's weakness.
- **Witch**: magic given by a mysterious patron through a familiar; curses and clever spells.
- **Wizard**: the scholar of magic; the most spells, carefully prepared.

**Not in this campaign:** Gunslinger. **Ask the DM first:** Exemplar (Rare), and anything Archives of Nethys marks as a playtest.

## Step 3 · Offer, don't list

Pick two or three. For each, say in two or three sentences **what they'd do on a turn** and **one honest trade-off**. Recommend one if you can, and say why. Then say there are more classes and offer the full list (Step 2 above, minus anything not allowed). If the player is completely new and torn, the **Fighter**, **Ranger** and **Champion** are the easiest to learn; casters have more to track.

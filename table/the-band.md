# The band so far

*For the Forge. These are the characters already built at this table, so you can **build the next one to fit the band, not to copy it**. This file holds only what the players themselves made. It says nothing about the campaign's world or anything secret. The DM updates it after every build.*

*How to use it:*
- *Read it before the opening.* It replaces the party card when it's newer.
- ***Never tell a player** what another character carries beyond the party-card line (their name, ancestry, class and role). The rest is for you to steer by, not to share. If a new player's idea runs close to what's here, say only: "Someone in the band already has a story close to that. Let's make yours its own."*
- *Steer away from **twins**: the same class and role, and also the same **motifs**: the same kind of loss, debt, enemy, family wound or source of dark magic. Two characters can share a theme only if they come at it from different sides, and say so to the DM.*
- *Steer toward what the band **lacks** (roles missing, and kinds of story not yet told).*

## Seat 1 · Ugo Fantozzi (Alessandro)

**In a line:** a hot-tempered gnome gambler and dark sorcerer with a devil's mark, the band's ranged blaster and silver tongue.

**His story, in short:** his father came home empty after two years taken by a lich lord, and sat silent on the porch; the family scattered. Ugo's dog was found turned to ash. Ugo later gambled away the house his father lives in and now works as a sellsword to pay a debt to a Tiefling gang boss that never seems to shrink. His brother Magnus is the one person he's afraid to lose. He hates the undead while wielding the magic of undeath.

**Motifs now taken. Steer new characters away from them, or come at them from a clearly different side:**
- a parent taken and returned broken; a family that scattered
- a lich lord, or any undead lord, as the enemy
- hating undead / undead or death magic
- a gambling debt; a debt to a gang or crime boss
- a devil's mark or fiendish touch (Nephilim)
- a beloved pet that died strangely
- the charming liar or "face" of the band
- a divine or arcane blaster at range

**What the band still has room for:** someone who fights up close, a healer, a scout; and stories that aren't about debt, undead or a broken father. A love, a calling, a rival, a crown, a cure, a homeland, a faith that works, a craft.

# The note to the DM

*For the Forge. Write it at the end of every build, in exactly this shape, so the DM can file it. Use the player's own words wherever you can.*

```
DM NOTE — [character name] · [player name] · built [date] · Forge v[version]

Ancestry / heritage / tier & points: …
Class (and level-1 choices) / role in the band: …
Background: …
Started from: [the player's own picture / a spark: name / nothing yet]

Portrait: [the full portrait]

THE HOOKS (what you can write from: names, places, when, alive or not)
  · Drives them:      [what they want to achieve or gain, and why]
  · At stake:         [who or what they still have and fear to lose]
  · Open wound:       [who or what is lost, missing, cursed or taken, with no ending]
  · Items:            [each object with its story; mark the one people notice]
  · Connections:
      People:         [name — who they are to the character — alive / dead / unknown] (mark the ally)
      Peoples, gods, devils, beasts: [what they feel, and why]
  · Hook seed taken:  [number and name, or "none"]

THE PERSON
  · The scene:      …
  · The count:      …
  · The line:       …
  · The crack:      …
  · Open question:  [left unanswered: the DM's to answer]

Chosen from my options: [anything the player asked me to choose, the options I gave, and what they picked]
Also said: [what they love · what they always do when trouble starts · a belief the world will test · anything else the player lit up at]
Not wanted in their story (if said): …
Casts magic? [no / yes: tradition and kind]
Flags for the DM: [anything not on the list, a companion or mount (what it is, its size), a flying familiar, Uncommon/Rare requests, Legacy content, Large size, rules I couldn't confirm, world questions the player asked]
Party card update: [one line for the card]
```
